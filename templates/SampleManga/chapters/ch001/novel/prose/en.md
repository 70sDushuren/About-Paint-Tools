Before dusk had fully faded, the wind-pressure sirens on Orbital Academy's north rooftop sounded their third warning.

Ren Hoshikawa pulled his uniform collar up to his chin, listening to the low static hum inside the gathering storm clouds. Three years ago, on the night of the first fall, the air had smelled just like this—wet iron and ozone. He had convinced himself that memory was buried, until the dormant hilt of the Starlight Blade pulsed warm against his palm.

"...So it's finally here."

Rather than drawing immediately, he shifted his stance in the azure flash of descending lightning, locking his eyes onto the first shard of orbital debris tearing through the clouds.
